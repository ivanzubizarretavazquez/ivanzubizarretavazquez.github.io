Iván Zubizarreta — cybersecurity

Security is not a wall. It's a living system.

This site is normally an interactive 3D journey through a digital network. Your device is showing the text version, which carries the same story.

01 — The network

Billions of connections.

Endpoints, servers, applications, identities and data moving between them. Every connection is a decision to trust something, and the map of those decisions is the real perimeter.

02 — The threat

Something changed.

Phishing redirects a path. Credential theft turns a known identity into an unknown one. Ransomware isolates whole clusters. A supply chain attack arrives signed and trusted. A zero-day appears where the system was considered stable.

03 — The attack

It only needs one path.

User, endpoint, application, server, database. Propagation is rarely loud: it looks like normal traffic until the moment it doesn't.

04 — The defense

Detection, containment, recovery.

Monitoring, identity, endpoint protection, network security, threat intelligence, incident response and zero trust are not products in a stack. They are one system that has to notice, decide and act faster than the attacker moves.

05 — The human

Behind every device, there is a person.

Identity, trust, awareness, human error. Cybersecurity protects people who are trying to do their job, not abstractions.
